"superscript single"

```````````````````````````````` example
This should be ^up^ above everything else.
.
<p>This should be <sup>up</sup> above everything else.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript double"

```````````````````````````````` example
This should be ^^up^^ above everything else.
.
<p>This should be <sup>up</sup> above everything else.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript triple"

```````````````````````````````` example
This should be ^^^up^^^ above everything else.
.
<p>This should be ^^^up^^^ above everything else.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript single character"

```````````````````````````````` example
x^2^
.
<p>x<sup>2</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with numbers"

```````````````````````````````` example
E=mc^2^
.
<p>E=mc<sup>2</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"multiple superscripts in one line"

```````````````````````````````` example
x^2^ + y^2^ = z^2^
.
<p>x<sup>2</sup> + y<sup>2</sup> = z<sup>2</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript at start of paragraph"

```````````````````````````````` example
^note^ This is important.
.
<p><sup>note</sup> This is important.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript at end of paragraph"

```````````````````````````````` example
See footnote^1^
.
<p>See footnote<sup>1</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with punctuation"

```````````````````````````````` example
Hello^world!^
.
<p>Hello<sup>world!</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with spaces"

```````````````````````````````` example
text ^with spaces^ more
.
<p>text <sup>with spaces</sup> more</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with special characters"

```````````````````````````````` example
math^2+3^
.
<p>math<sup>2+3</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript adjacent to text"

```````````````````````````````` example
test^ing^test
.
<p>test<sup>ing</sup>test</p>
````````````````````````````````

"empty superscript"

```````````````````````````````` example
text^^text
.
<p>text^^text</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with markdown inside"

```````````````````````````````` example
text ^**bold**^
.
<p>text <sup><strong>bold</strong></sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with code inside"

```````````````````````````````` example
text ^`code`^
.
<p>text <sup><code>code</code></sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

"escaped caret should not be superscript"

```````````````````````````````` example
text \^not superscript\^
.
<p>text ^not superscript^</p>
````````````````````````````````

"unmatched opening caret"

```````````````````````````````` example
text ^not closed
.
<p>text ^not closed</p>
````````````````````````````````

"unmatched closing caret"

```````````````````````````````` example
text not opened^
.
<p>text not opened^</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript in list item"

```````````````````````````````` example
- Item with ^superscript^
.
<ul>
<li>Item with <sup>superscript</sup></li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript in blockquote"

```````````````````````````````` example
> Quote with ^superscript^
.
<blockquote>
<p>Quote with <sup>superscript</sup></p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

"nested superscript"

```````````````````````````````` example
x^y^z^
.
<p>x<sup>y</sup>z^</p>
````````````````````````````````

"superscript with caret inside"

```````````````````````````````` example
text ^caret ^ inside^
.
<p>text <sup>caret ^ inside</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````
