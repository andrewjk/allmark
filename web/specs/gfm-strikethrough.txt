"spec strikethrough"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~Hi~~ Hello, world!
.
<p><del>Hi</del> Hello, world!</p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough single word"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~deleted~~
.
<p><del>deleted</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough multiple words"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~this is deleted~~
.
<p><del>this is deleted</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with spaces inside"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~  spaces  ~~
.
<p>~~  spaces  ~~</p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with emphasis"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~*bold and deleted*~~
.
<p><del><em>bold and deleted</em></del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough inside emphasis"

```````````````````````````````` example
*~~deleted in italic~~*
.
<p><em><del>deleted in italic</del></em></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with code"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~code: `var x` here~~
.
<p><del>code: <code>var x</code> here</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with link"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~[link text](http://example.com)~~
.
<p><del><a href="http://example.com">link text</a></del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"multiple strikethroughs in one line"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~first~~ and ~~second~~ and ~~third~~
.
<p><del>first</del> and <del>second</del> and <del>third</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough at start of paragraph"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~deleted~~ followed by normal text.
.
<p><del>deleted</del> followed by normal text.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough at end of paragraph"

```````````````````````````````` example
Normal text followed by ~~deleted~~
.
<p>Normal text followed by <del>deleted</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough in list item"

```````````````````````````````` example
- ~~deleted item~~
- normal item
.
<ul>
<li><del>deleted item</del></li>
<li>normal item</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with tildes inside"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~text with ~ tilde~~
.
<p><del>text with ~ tilde</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with multiple tildes"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~~~double~~~~
.
<pre><code class="language-double~~~~"></code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough across lines"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~line one
line two~~
.
<p><del>line one
line two</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with punctuation"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~Hello, world!~~
.
<p><del>Hello, world!</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with numbers"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~12345~~
.
<p><del>12345</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough in table cell"

```````````````````````````````` example
| col1 | col2 |
| ---- | ---- |
| ~~deleted~~ | normal 
.
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>col1</th>
<th>col2</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><del>deleted</del></td>
<td>normal</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough adjacent to regular text"

```````````````````````````````` example
normal~~deleted~~normal
.
<p>normal<del>deleted</del>normal</p>
````````````````````````````````

"strikethrough with escaped characters"

```````````````````````````````` example
~~text with \*asterisk\*~~
.
<p><del>text with *asterisk*</del></p>
````````````````````````````````
