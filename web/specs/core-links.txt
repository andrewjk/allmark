"basic inline link"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google](https://google.com)
.
<p><a href="https://google.com">Google</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link with title"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google](https://google.com "Search Engine")
.
<p><a href="https://google.com" title="Search Engine">Google</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link with single quoted title"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google](https://google.com 'Search Engine')
.
<p><a href="https://google.com" title="Search Engine">Google</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link in paragraph"

```````````````````````````````` example
Visit [Google](https://google.com) for search.
.
<p>Visit <a href="https://google.com">Google</a> for search.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"multiple links in one line"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google](https://google.com) and [GitHub](https://github.com)
.
<p><a href="https://google.com">Google</a> and <a href="https://github.com">GitHub</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link with emphasis"

```````````````````````````````` example
[*Google*](https://google.com)
.
<p><a href="https://google.com"><em>Google</em></a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"emphasis around link"

```````````````````````````````` example
*[Google](https://google.com)*
.
<p><em><a href="https://google.com">Google</a></em></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link with code in text"

```````````````````````````````` example
[`const`](https://example.com)
.
<p><a href="https://example.com"><code>const</code></a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link in list item"

```````````````````````````````` example
- [Link](https://example.com)
.
<ul>
<li><a href="https://example.com">Link</a></li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

"link in heading"

```````````````````````````````` example
# See [Google](https://google.com)
.
<h1>See <a href="https://google.com">Google</a></h1>
````````````````````````````````

"reference link definition and usage"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google][google]

[google]: https://google.com
.
<p><a href="https://google.com">Google</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"reference link with implicit label"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google][]

[Google]: https://google.com
.
<p><a href="https://google.com">Google</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"reference link with title"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google][google]

[google]: https://google.com "Search Engine"
.
<p><a href="https://google.com" title="Search Engine">Google</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"multiple reference links"

```````````````````````````````` example
[Google][google] and [GitHub][github]

[google]: https://google.com
[github]: https://github.com
.
<p><a href="https://google.com">Google</a> and <a href="https://github.com">GitHub</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
<http://example.com>
````````````````````````````````

"autolink with https"

```````````````````````````````` example
<https://example.com>
.
<p><a href="https://example.com">https://example.com</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"autolink with ftp"

```````````````````````````````` example
<ftp://example.com>
.
<p><a href="ftp://example.com">ftp://example.com</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"email autolink"

```````````````````````````````` example
<user@example.com>
.
<p><a href="mailto:user@example.com">user@example.com</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

"link with special characters in URL"

```````````````````````````````` example

.
````````````````````````````````
