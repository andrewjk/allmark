"deletion single"

```````````````````````````````` example
This text was {-deleted-} recently.
.
<p>This text was <del class="markdown-deletion">deleted</del> recently.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"deletion double"

```````````````````````````````` example
This text was {--deleted--} recently.
.
<p>This text was <del class="markdown-deletion">deleted</del> recently.</p>
````````````````````````````````

"deletion triple"

```````````````````````````````` example
This text was {---deleted---} recently.
.
<p>This text was {---deleted---} recently.</p>
````````````````````````````````
